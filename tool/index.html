Centers Explanation Program

Offline report builder — select centers, channel types/circuits, and members, then download a Word report.

How to use

Turn on centers and/or channel type & circuit toggles, plus members. With members selected, center sections cover only activated gates in those charts. With centers selected and no members (and no type/circuit toggles), Report builds a general center reference: every gate of each selected center, channels, Throat voices, and condensed lines. Channel type and circuit sections stand on their own and list each selected member’s completed channels. The .docx keeps the approved condensed style (title header, centered Page X of Y footer, H1/H2/H3).
